Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

Who runs FatRaisin

FatRaisin is a personal, independent app for finding Canadian wineries, breweries, distilleries and cideries, and for keeping track of your own wine cellar. It is run by one person (“the owner”), not a company, and isn’t affiliated with any winery, brewery, distillery, Google or Apple. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

What stays on your device

Your favourites, route plan, cellar list, label and place photos, corrected map pins, and app settings are saved in your browser’s storage on your own device. If you aren’t signed in, none of this leaves your device except as described under “Services the app uses” below.

If you sign in

Accounts use Firebase Authentication, a Google service. If you sign in with email and password, Firebase receives your email address and a secure version of your password. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app. This is used only to show who is signed in and to sync your data. When you’re signed in, your favourites, route plan and cellar list (but not photos) are stored in a Firestore database (also Google) under your account so they appear on any device you sign into. Only your signed-in account can read or change your data through the app. As the Firebase project administrator, the owner can technically see stored account data, but only looks at it to fix problems or to delete data you ask to have deleted.

Services the app uses

Some features send information to outside services, only when you use them:
• Google Maps and Places: the map, place opening hours, websites, reviews and photos, and map-pin corrections send a place’s name and address to Google.
• Google Cloud Vision: when you photograph a wine label, the photo is sent to Google to read its text and find matching pictures of the bottle online. The owner doesn’t keep a copy.
• Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to this free product database.
• FatRaisin web lookup: to find a wine’s official page, the wine’s name, producer and vintage (or a web link you paste) are sent to the owner’s small helper on Cloudflare. It searches the web through Serper (Google search results) and reads the wine’s public web page. Answers are kept for one day so the same search isn’t repeated. No personal information is sent.
• Driving routes: route lines use the free public OSRM routing service, which receives the coordinates of your stops (and your location if you choose to start from it).
• Links: directions, websites, Instagram, OpenTable, Tock and other links open those services, under their own privacy terms.
Each of these services handles the information under its own privacy policy.

Camera and location

The camera is used only when you tap to scan a barcode or photograph a label. Live camera video is read on your device and isn’t recorded or uploaded; only label photos you take are sent to Google Cloud Vision as described above. Your location is used on your device to show distances, nearby places and routes. It isn’t stored on any server, and shared route links never include anyone’s location.

What the app doesn’t do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking, and your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

Age

FatRaisin is about alcoholic drinks and is meant for people of legal drinking age where they live (19 in most of Canada; 18 in Alberta, Manitoba and Quebec).

Deleting your data

Data on your device is removed by clearing your browser’s website data for FatRaisin. To delete your account and everything synced to it, contact the owner (below) and it will be deleted.
