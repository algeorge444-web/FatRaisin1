Help & Support

FatRaisin, a free guide to Canadian wineries, breweries, distilleries and cideries

Contact

Questions, problems, or a place or wine that’s wrong? Email algeorge444@gmail.com and I’ll get back to you.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse places, events and the map without one. Signing in (email, Google or Apple) saves your favourites, route and wine cellar so they appear on any device.

Adding wines to your cellar

Open the Cellar tab and tap Snap the label (front and back labels work best), Scan barcode, or Add by hand. You can also add wines from the shared catalogue.

Wrong or closed places

Email the place’s name and what’s wrong, or use Suggest a place on the Account tab to send a new one.

Deleting your account

Sign in, open the Account tab and tap Delete my account. Your account and everything synced to it are deleted straight away. You can also email the address above to have it deleted.

Your location

Your location is only used on your device to show what’s near you. It isn’t stored or shared.
