FatRaisin

FatRaisin is a free guide to Canadian and US wineries, breweries, distilleries and cideries.

With FatRaisin you can find places near you, see opening hours, events and directions, plan a tasting route and share it with friends, save favourites, and keep track of the wines in your own cellar by photographing their labels. You can browse without an account; signing in with email or Google only syncs your favourites and cellar between your devices.
